Fewer effects that are not there: also new in build 0.4.2

Up to build 0.4.1 the app showed an effect as soon as one short stretch of the sound looked like it. That put 1.6 effects per sound on the screen that were not there. Now every effect is checked against the whole sound before it is shown. On test sounds it was not tuned on, 93 of 100 clean sounds come out with none of those effects shown; before it was 12 of 100. On the 136 test cases with an outside effect the groups shown that are not the case's effect went from 156 to 12, leaving REVERB aside.

The price: flanger, phaser, filter sweep and a fixed filter are found less often than before, because most of their earlier finds came with as many false ones. The flanger has an engine of its own since build 0.4.3, chorus, phaser and filter sweep since build 0.4.4, ring modulation and stutter since build 0.4.7, and the fixed filter since build 0.5.1 (all above). Turning Sureness down shows more, turning it up shows less, and it now means the same for every effect.

REVERB also showed less often where there is none. On a sound that is cut off while it is still playing, the reverb engine used to read a room into nearly half of the dry test sounds. Build 0.4.2 patched that with a second look with silence after the sound, which a cut sound with a real reverb paid for. Build 0.4.5 replaces that patch with a model that has learned such files (above).

What comes next

With build 0.5.2 the declipper you picked is in. Nothing further is started: what comes next is yours to pick. Noted from this round: where a cut's tops are not rebuilt, the cut goes onto a second sound too deep, because how hard a dense sound was driven is read badly; clippers that work at a higher sample rate inside; and cut levels that differ between left and right. Still noted from before: the REVERB group that still comes up on some filtered, saturated and chorused sounds without a room; a soft saturation, which is mostly not shown; the phaser's next step; for the Pitch fader, sounds that bounce up and down, two things that move at once, and reading a sound a second time once its read movement is out; a crusher with a filter after it or with a wet/dry mix near the middle; the first milliseconds of a Haas delay in a file cut from something longer; and for the reverb engine the two repairs from the last round and a larger model.
