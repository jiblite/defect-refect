Level movement and flanger: new in build 0.4.3

TREMOLO, AUTO PAN and GATED RHYTHM have a new engine. All three are one thing: a gain that moves in a steady beat, for a tremolo on both sides together, for an auto pan one side against the other, for a gate in hard steps. The engine looks for a gain that moves every band of the sound by the same amount at the same moment and repeats exactly, which notes and hits do not. Taking the movement out is dividing by that gain, and putting it on a second sound is multiplying by it, in the first sound's beat. Speed makes it slower or faster.

FLANGER has a new engine too. A flanger is the sound plus a copy of itself whose delay moves. The engine follows the copy's delay through the sound and reads how loud the copy is and how much of it is fed back. Taking it out is the exact opposite filter, and the same moving filter goes onto a second sound. Speed, Sweep and Feedback change the movement.

Both engines work on long sounds, and both say where along the sound the effect is. An effect that is on a part of the sound only is taken out of that part, and the rest comes back as it went in. The app says from when to when and draws a line under the waveform. A level movement goes onto the second sound at the same times; a flanger goes onto all of it.

The four flangers that are found come out at 96, 91, 89 and 39. On 198 clean test sounds none of these four groups is shown.

Long sounds, 20 to 62 seconds. Level movement: on 88 sounds with the effect on all of the sound it is shown on 81 and taken out at tremolo 88, auto pan 94, gate 74 (typical cases). On 44 sounds with the effect on one half it is shown on 36 and taken out at 80 typically, none comes out worse than it went in, and the half without the effect comes back exactly as it went in on 31 of those 36. Slow pans, 3 to 23 seconds a sweep, are shown on 21 of 24 and taken out at 94; the other three pass only about once in their sound and are offered when Sureness is turned down. On 48 clean long sounds nothing is shown.

A second draw of long sounds gives the same picture: shown on 76 of 88 and 39 of 44, taken out at tremolo 86, auto pan 95, gate 71, none worse than it went in, nothing shown on 48 clean ones. That second draw also brought out three faults with gates, which were fixed before this build. On a third draw, made after the fixes, gates come out at 54 on a whole sound and at 68 on one half; the code before the fixes had 45 and 52 there. Two things went wrong on that third draw: one tremolo comes out worse than it went in, and one gated sound is shown as a slow auto pan instead of a gate.

Flanger on long sounds: found less often. On 24 flanged sounds it is shown on 3 (taken out at 93, 85 and 30) and on 3 more when Sureness is turned down, and none comes out worse than it went in. On 24 sounds flanged in one half it is shown on 4 (taken out at 75, 65 and 63; one comes out a little worse than it went in), and the half without the flanger comes back as it went in. On 24 clean long sounds no flanger is shown.

Limits: a tremolo of a few dB on a busy sound is often not shown at the default Sureness, and neither is a sawtooth that falls, because a level that jumps up and sinks back is what struck notes do. Gates are the hardest of the three to take out. Light flangers on busy sounds and thru-zero flangers are not found, and on long sounds a flanger is found less often than on short ones. Chorus, phaser and filter sweep still wait for engines of their own.
