Echoes: new in build 0.4.2

The DELAY group has a new engine. An echo is the sound plus later copies of itself, and those copies leave a regular ripple in the sound's spectrum. The engine reads the echoes straight from that ripple: when they come on each side, how loud each repeat is and what tone it has. Taking the echoes out is then the exact opposite filter, and putting them on a second sound is the same filter the other way round. Time, Feedback and Brightness reshape the echoes, the display shows them, and Export IR saves them as an impulse response.

On the eight test cases with a delay from an outside effect the app now finds the echo in seven, takes 89 of 100 out and puts 95 of 100 onto the second sound. Build 0.4.1 found six, with 54 and 50. The case it does not show has a very quiet echo, a tenth of the sound's level. Limits: one echo time per side, a quiet echo on a busy sound often shows only with Sureness turned down, and echoes shorter than 30 ms are left to the COMB FILTER group.
