39. A crusher and a stutter in a file at 44.1 kHz

A trombone melody, as a file at 44.1 kHz. On it: a crusher with 256 levels that holds every value for 3 samples (14.7 kHz), and after it a stutter with four slices of 45 to 250 ms, played 3 to 8 times. Both engines read such a file at its own rate, because the change to 48 kHz that the rest of the app works at takes the values off their grid and makes the repeats unequal. The app found the sound rounded to 256 levels (8 bits), each value held for 3 samples (14.7 kHz). The app found 3 slices of 60 to 250 ms, each played 3 to 4 times. The slice of 45 ms, played 8 times, fell on a rest between two notes. The crusher rounds that rest to pure silence, and silence played eight times cannot be told from a longer rest, so the app reads three of the four; on the second sound that one stutter is missing, which the score counts. Score for putting both on the second sound: 73.
