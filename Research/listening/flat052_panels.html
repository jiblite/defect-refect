52. A sound cut off flat

A sample loop. The real effect: a plain overload. The sound was turned up by 11.2 dB against full scale, so its loudest tops stood 11.2 dB above the ceiling, and what did not fit was cut off: 11 % of all its values. The app found the cut, rebuilt the tops from the sound around them, and reads that the loudest of them stood 11.2 dB above the cut. Before it rebuilds a sound, the app tests itself on that sound: it cuts tops that are whole and rebuilds those. Here the test scored 29 of 100, and from 20 on the tops are rebuilt. Scores for taking the effect out: build 0.5.1 −68, this build 44. Build 0.5.1 showed this sound as SATURATION. The cut goes onto a second sound by how high the rebuilt tops stand: that sound is pushed as far past the cut. Here the height was read almost to the tenth of a dB, and the second sound scores 100 (build 0.5.1: 28). On the six test cases of this kind it scores between 32 and 100, 72 on average (build 0.5.1: 13).

53. A distortion that presses the tops flat

Single notes (church organ). The real effect: a plain distortion, one curve and nothing else, driven by 23.3 dB. It does not cut the tops off, it presses them nearly flat: 49 % of all values sit in such tops. Measured on the sound before and after the effect, the loudest values were squeezed by 15.1 dB; the app reads 17.2 dB. The app found the pressed tops. It rebuilt them from the sound around them, read the curve off what it had rebuilt, and with the curve known rebuilt them a second time, which is what far left gives. Scores for taking the effect out: build 0.5.1 −10, this build 80. On a second sound the curve is put on as in build 0.5.1 (95).

55. A dense sound that is left alone

A drum kit pattern. The real effect: a hard clipper, driven by 10.8 dB, that cuts 4.8 % of all values off flat. The app found the cut and tested itself on this sound: it cut tops that are whole and rebuilt those, and scored 15 of 100. That is under the 20 it asks for, because this sound is dense and what a top was cannot be told from the sound around it. So nothing is rebuilt, and left of the middle the fader leaves the sound as it is. Build 0.5.1 walked a bend back here that cannot be walked back that way. Scores for taking the effect out: build 0.5.1 −283, this build 0.

56. The case that loses most against build 0.5.1

A tabla pattern. The real effect: a soft clipper that works at four times the sample rate inside, driven by 19.1 dB. Such a clipper leaves tops that are flat with a ripple on them, which is harder to read than a curve's work. Measured on the sound before and after the effect, the loudest values were squeezed by 6.8 dB; the app reads 11 dB. It rebuilds the tops, and its self-test gave it 43 of 100. The result lies further from the sound before the effect than build 0.5.1's. Scores for taking the effect out: build 0.5.1 19, this build −60. Build 0.5.1 showed this sound as SATURATION. Of the 63 cases of the trial whose tops are rebuilt this is the one that loses most. The effect changed this sound only a little, so the score swings widely for a small difference: your ears say more here than the number.
