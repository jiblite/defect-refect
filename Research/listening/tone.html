Tone: new in build 0.4.1

The tone engine does two things. On one sound it finds short reflections and takes them out with the exact opposite filter. With two sounds, the Tone Match button gives the second sound the tone of the first, as a curve you can see, shape with Amount and Detail, and save with Export IR.

What it cannot do is find a plain EQ on one sound alone. I measured this: from the outside an EQ and the sound's own colour look the same, and every rule I tried did more harm than good.

In examples 8 and 9 the other effect groups were switched off (each group now has an On/Off switch). On these sounds the app also showed a filter and a filter sweep that are not there, and with those left on the result is worse than doing nothing. Fewer such false finds is the next piece of work.
