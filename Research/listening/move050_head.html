Movement: Pitch, Loudness, Tone, Stereo and Spectral. New in build 0.5.0

This is the group you asked for. MOVEMENT is always on the screen, before any found effect, with five faders: Pitch, Loudness, Tone, Stereo, and the Spectral fader you added. All five run the same way. Far left is flat: the movement is taken out. The middle is the sound's own, and there nothing is done at all: the sound comes out exactly as it went in. Far right with one sound is the sound's own movement twice. With two sounds the group works on the second sound, and far right puts the first sound's movement in the place of its own.

Pitch is how the pitch moves: a rev, a glide, a pass-by, a vibrato. It is put on like tape speed. The sound is read at a moving place, nothing is stretched or built anew, so nothing warbles and nothing gets dull, and the sound keeps its length. Loudness is the level, read every 5 ms. Tone is how the balance of the sound's bands shifts over time, and Spectral is the colour the sound has on average. Stereo is where the sound sits between the sides and how wide it is.

Pitch decided this build. You called the pitch changes of an earlier demo bad, most of all on the engine, so the reader of pitch movement has one rule above all others: where it cannot read, its curve stands still. It never fills a passage with a guess, and what it does not read stays in the sound. Holding that rule on your recordings took nine versions of the reader. The eighth still stepped away and back in 11 places of 29 recordings, by 90 cents and more, where it took a new sound to start and the sound in truth ran on. The ninth leaves no step at any of the 13 places of that kind. The price is that it reads less: a short piece of sound beside a held one is now left alone.

What it does not read, said plainly. Of your 40 recordings the pitch is followed over four fifths of the sound or more in 11, over two fifths to four fifths in 9, over less than that in 11, and hardly at all in 9, which are noise-like. So on about half of your recordings the Pitch fader does little or nothing, and where it reads nothing at all it says so ("no pitch"). Left unread: noise; an engine whose partials lie closer than about 25 Hz while it revs fast; the very top of a fast rev and a drop from one moment to the next; a short sound beside a held one; of a sound that bounces up and down only a part; and where two things move at once, one of them or neither.

The other four faders on test sounds with a known movement: Loudness at far left brings a swell, a pass-by, a fade or steps of 4 to 6 dB down to 0.1 to 0.5 dB and leaves the single pulses of a low engine alone. Stereo at far left brings a pan sweep of 7 to 13 dB down to 0.3 dB or less, and the level changes by less than 0.5 dB. Tone reads a tilt or a sweep of 1.5 to 3.6 dB to within 0.3 to 0.8 dB (1.6 for a narrow bump that sweeps through a chord) and holds it about as still as a steady sound is by itself. Spectral at far left brings a colouring of 3 to 8 dB against neutral down to 0.5 to 1.8 dB, and at far right it brings a second sound to within 0.8 to 1.3 dB of the first sound's colour.

Nothing else in the app has changed. With the MOVEMENT faders in the middle, 38 test cases, two of every effect type, come out the same as in build 0.4.7 to the last bit, alone and on a second sound.
