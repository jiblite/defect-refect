This is the V2 reverb engine as it runs since build 0.4.1, with the tone engine and the echo engine of build 0.4.2 further down. After them comes what is new in build 0.4.3, the engines for level movement (tremolo, auto pan, gated rhythm) and for the flanger, then what is new in build 0.4.4, the engines for chorus, phaser and filter sweep (examples 18 to 23), and last what is new in build 0.4.5: the reverb engine on files that stop while the sound plays, and fewer reverbs shown that are not there (examples 24 to 28). The reverb engine hears one sound and decides, band by band and moment by moment, what is room and what is the sound itself. From that it reads the room (decay time, level, width) and can turn the room down or up on that sound, or put the same room on a second sound.

Every reverb here comes from an effect that is not my code, and the engine never gets the dry sound. Examples 1 to 6 and the first table are as build 0.4.1 made them; what build 0.4.5 changes is in the last section. Headphones help.
